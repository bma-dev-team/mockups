Install on your phone

Put Spoken on your home screen.

It opens full screen like any app, with the player controls on your lock screen and headphones.

AC 15 installable on iOS and Android AC 16 one stable address

iPhone

In Safari, from your Spoken address:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Spoken appears with the other apps.

Android

In Chrome, from your Spoken address:

  1. Tap Install in the bar that appears at the bottom, or
  2. Open the three-dot menu and choose Install app.
  3. Tap Install. Spoken appears with the other apps.
Your Spoken address is given to you at delivery. It does not change. Bookmark it, or install from it and forget it.

Spoken itself opens instantly once installed. Adding documents and hearing them read needs a connection, because the voices are generated on the server.
