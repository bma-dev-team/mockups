How these numbers are worked out
  • Your predicted score gives a margin and a total. Your chance of a moneyline, spread or total bet winning assumes the real result lands around your prediction, spread out the way NFL and NBA results usually are.
  • Touchdowns, receptions, rebounds, assists and threes are counts, worked out with a standard model for counts. Yards and points use the same spread-out model as the score.
  • Expected value is your chance times the payout at the best price among your books, minus your stake. A boost raises the payout.
  • The suggested stake is a quarter of the Kelly amount, never more than 5% of your bankroll.
  • Parlay legs are treated as independent, which is why each leg comes from a different game.
